# Generation-bound raw IRQ scheduler stacks — experimental, HOLD

The exact experimental compiler is Hepta #962, 846530249b26bf70bb8bb2fe52f485486a0d5188. The new raw operation dispatch_gen_stack has a separate ABI; existing 0/1 entry bytes and all inherited gates remain unchanged. It acquires the descriptor's even generation with locked CAS, retains the acquired odd word in callee-preserved RBP, rechecks GS after the callback, and releases exactly that odd word with CAS before IRET. Odd and terminal even words fail closed, with no wraparound. The HIA callback binds the held word to 2*epoch+1, performs checked EOI, and advances the epoch using exact CAS.

Two real QEMU CPUs take overlapping interrupts on separate scheduler stacks, each executing two deliveries with exact lease progression 0->1->2->3->4 and epoch 2. The existing stack extent, guard canaries, registers, same-frame RSP/RIP restoration and live CPU binding are retained. A real AP IRQ targeting the BSP descriptor while the BSP is held in its callback must trap at the precise busy guard; its own descriptor stays free and BSP lease/epochs remain unchanged. This is an actual shared-slot contention refusal, not a simulated CAS or general mapping authority.

Independent fixture checks cover busy, retired, last valid cycle then actual terminal refusal, post-callback GS mutation, stale held-word mutation, and four individual machine-code guard-bypass negatives. Each checked native trap has an exact linked instruction address, phase-specific state and ISR checks. Local PC/Q35: 22/22 in focus-r3, with 20/20 r2 preserved. Both source/object and double empty-PATH builds are checked; the observer is not the IRQ implementation. Inherited workflow reruns 392 cases and adds 22, for 414; CI outcome is pending at publication.

Not complete SMP task migration, arbitrary CPU/page ownership, simultaneous arbitrary mappings, scheduler policy, production panic handling, NMI nesting, FPU, CPL3 or error-code entry. Both production mains, fixed Linux target, lock, budgets and original 17 HOLD gates and 422 syscall obligations remain unchanged. Prior failures remain in the ledger.
